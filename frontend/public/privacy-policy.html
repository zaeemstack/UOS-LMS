Privacy Policy

Application: UOS Attendance (University of Sahiwal Academic Attendance Management Portal)
Last Updated: November 2026

The University of Sahiwal ("we", "our", or "the University") is committed to protecting the privacy of its faculty, instructors, and students. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and safeguarding across our mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide secure academic attendance management, the application processes the following types of information:

  • Faculty & User Account Credentials: Full Name, Official University Email Address, Employee ID / Roll Number, and encrypted password hash.
  • Academic Attendance Data: Timetable slot IDs, course codes, classroom designations, date, time of marking, and student attendance statuses (Present, Absent, Late).
  • Network Security Data: Client IP address, connection subnet, and network audit status (to enforce campus Wi-Fi perimeter security and prevent remote attendance proxy fraud).

🔒 Biometric Privacy Protection Guarantee:
The application DOES NOT collect, store, or transmit raw biometric data (such as fingerprint images or facial geometry templates). All biometric authentications are handled strictly locally by your smartphone's secure hardware (Android BiometricPrompt / iOS LocalAuthentication). Our servers only receive a one-time cryptographic success confirmation to certify instructor presence.

2. How Information is Used

The collected information is used solely for official academic and university governance purposes:

  • Certifying faculty presence and student class attendance.
  • Preventing proxy attendance and remote unauthorized logins.
  • Generating official university attendance registers, delinquency reports, and administrative analytics.
  • Evaluating class lock timers and processing HOD attendance override requests.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share any personal, academic, or network data with any third-party advertisers, commercial entities, or external analytics firms. All data remains strictly within the University of Sahiwal internal servers.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard cryptographic and architectural security controls:

  • All network communications between mobile devices and backend servers use encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections.
  • Passwords are hashed with one-way salted bcrypt algorithms.
  • Rate limiting and automated lockout protections prevent brute-force credential attacks.
  • Database records are stored within protected university database engines with restricted administrative access.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Academic attendance records are retained according to the University of Sahiwal's official academic record retention policies. Instructors or students wishing to review or request corrections to attendance records may submit a formal request to their respective Department Head or Academic Registrar.
